# Reviewed PEAs — What an Evaluator Flags

> When to read: seeing the evaluation criteria applied to real articles.
> Sources: Cargill & O'Connor (2009) Ch. 18–19 (PEAs) and Ch. 3/13 (referee
> form).

Below, the two Provided Example Articles are read **as a referee would read
them**. Each block shows what a referee would flag and under which criterion
(numbered from `referee-criteria.md`).

## PEA 1 — Kaiser et al. (2003)

*Citation: Kaiser, B.N. et al. (2003). The soybean NRAMP homologue, GmDMT1,
is a symbiotic divalent metal transporter capable of ferrous iron transport.
The Plant Journal 35, 295–304.*

### Title (criterion 11) — PASS
- Statement form, keywords front-loaded (*soybean*, *NRAMP*, *GmDMT1*).
- Indicates the result explicitly ("is a symbiotic divalent metal transporter
  capable of ferrous iron transport").
- No ambiguity in noun phrases.

### Abstract (criteria 11, 19, 20) — PASS
- All five elements present (B/P/M/R/C).
- Conclusion hedged with "indicate" — matches the evidence.
- No references, figures, or judgemental statements.

### Introduction (criteria 1, 2, 3, 4) — PASS
- Stage 1 broad setting established.
- Stage 3 gap signalled: "The proteins involved in this transport have not
  yet been identified."
- Stage 4 aim consistent with Methods/Results/Discussion wording.
- Information-prominent citation style throughout — appropriate for the field.

### Methods (criteria 5, 8) — PASS
- Subheadings (*Plant growth*, *Isolation of GmDmt1;1*, *Northern analysis*,
  *Antibody generation…*, *Symbiosome isolation…*, *Functional expression in
  yeast*) mirror the Results topics.
- Enough detail for reproducibility (buffer compositions, centrifugation
  forces, primer sequences).
- Passive voice used to avoid "we" monotony.

### Results (criteria 5, 8, 9, 10) — PASS
- Figures carry the localisation evidence; prose highlights, doesn't repeat.
- Combined highlight+location sentences ("…(Figure 3a)…").
- No commentary — interpretation saved for the Discussion.

### Discussion (criterion 6) — PASS (the model case)
- Claim strength calibrated precisely:
  - "demonstrate that GmDmt1;1 is a symbiotically enhanced homologue…" —
    strong, supported by the sequence + localisation + functional data.
  - "it **appears** that GmDmt1;1 **has** the capacity…" — moderate; the
    in-vivo direction was not directly shown.
  - "The presence of an IRE motif **suggests** that GmDmt1;1 mRNA **may be
    stabilised**…" — weak; a hypothesis for future work.
- Diplomatic disagreement with Grotz & Guerinot (2002) on AtNramp
  localisation — lets the authors' own data make the contrast.

### What a referee would likely comment on
- **Minor:** the *fet3fet4* complementation was "not robust" — the authors
  pre-empt this honestly in their own Discussion. A referee may ask for one
  more sentence explaining the supplemented-iron condition.
- **Minor:** reference list formatting (abbreviations) — criterion 12.

## PEA 2 — Britton-Simmons & Abbott (2008)

*Citation: Britton-Simmons, K.H. & Abbott, K.C. (2008). Short- and long-term
effects of disturbance and propagule pressure on a biological invasion.
Journal of Ecology 96, 68–77.*

### Title (criterion 11) — PASS
- Noun phrase form, keywords front-loaded.
- Signals two time scales — a distinctive contribution.

### Abstract (criteria 19, 20) — PASS (exemplary)
- Numbered format per *Journal of Ecology* requirement, with the final
  **Synthesis** point.
- Synthesis accessible to non-specialists — maximises impact, as the journal
  requests.

### Introduction (criteria 1, 2, 4) — PASS (exemplary)
- Every paragraph opens with a topic sentence that predicts its content.
- Stage 3 gap signalled with multiple signal words: *however*, *remains a
  major challenge*, *rarely*, *not well understood*, *presently unclear*.
- Stage 4 explicitly matches the dual experimental + modelling design.

### Methods (criteria 5, 8) — PASS
- Subheadings (STUDY SYSTEM, THE INVADER, FIELD EXPERIMENT, STATISTICAL
  ANALYSIS, MODEL) map onto Results.
- Active/passive mixed deliberately to keep old information at the front of
  each sentence.

### Results (criteria 8, 9, 10) — PASS
- Figure 1 legend contains all five parts (title, what's shown, additional
  context, units/statistics, symbols).
- Prose highlights the interaction — no commentary.

### Discussion (criterion 6) — PASS
- Claim strength scaled:
  - "**demonstrate** that space- and propagule-limitation both **regulate**…"
    — strong; earned by the factorial experiment.
  - "**indicate** that… **will be determined** by the interaction…" — strong
    for the model's prediction.
- The Synthesis point restates the take-home message in plain terms.

### What a referee would likely comment on
- **Substantive (potential criterion 5):** model parameters with wide
  uncertainty ranges — a referee may ask for a sensitivity-analysis
  paragraph.
- **Minor:** data at very low and very high available space, but little in
  between (the authors acknowledge this and use model averaging to handle it).

## How to use these annotations

- When pre-reviewing a draft, work through the same criteria in the same
  order. Note PASS / flag / minor.
- The pattern to imitate: **claim strength drops precisely where evidence is
  indirect**, and **topic sentences predict their paragraph's content**.
- The pattern to avoid: commentary in Results, top-heavy Methods sentences,
  over-claimed conclusions.
